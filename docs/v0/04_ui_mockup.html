Annotations
Notes on each element · read alongside the data dictionary
1

Summary strip

Five numbers, large type. Record is W–L (voids shown as sub-metric). Total PnL and ROI are computed from v_summary. Hit rate is won/settled; implied is the average entry YES price across settled bets — if the rule is well-calibrated, hit rate should track implied over a large enough sample.

2

Equity curve

A single line: cumulative PnL over time, one point per settled bet. Sourced from v_equity_curve. Zero is the dashed baseline. No moving averages, no shaded confidence bands — just the line. Daily x-axis labels only.

3

Bet ledger

Every paper bet, newest first. Sourced from v_bet_detail. Pending bets at the top are today's slate (already locked at T-30, awaiting game result). Scrolls. No pagination in v0.

4

Matchup & pick

Both teams shown with an abstracted color tile (team abbreviation in team colors, no licensed logos in v0). The amber pick callout is the team the bot bet YES on — always the favorite.

5

Entry price

The Kalshi YES price in cents at T-30min, as recorded in market_snapshots. For the favorite, this is the higher-priced YES contract of the two per game. A 62¢ entry on a $10 stake buys ~16.1 contracts; a win pays ~$16.13 (PnL +$6.13), a loss pays $0 (PnL −$10.00).

—

What is deliberately not on this screen

No pitcher. No recent form. No lineup. No injury notes. No NO-side, no spreads, no props. No strategy controls. No "place bet" button. All parking lot — v0 is a calculator, not a cockpit.
